QNA

What is a webring?

A webring seeks to connect people from around the web with a shared interest, goal, or in this case, identity.

I did not know of any webrings specifically for queer people of color around, and decided to take matters into my own hands and create one. I've often struggled to find others that share this part of my identity, and being both queer and a person of color is important to me. I hope this webring is able to connect others that share this intersection.

Does this webring accept...?

Sites containing mature content: Yes. You may request that your site be marked as 18+ in your submission at your own discretion. This option is available even if your site does not contain mature content.

Pages made using a site builder: Yes, as long as your page is able to place the widget and serves as more than just a repository of links.

Project and organization pages: No, this webring is focused on personal sites.

Am I considered a queer person of color?

That is for you to decide. If you feel that your identity aligns with being a queer person of color, you are welcome. It is not in my interest to police anyone for not fitting in exactly in strictly defined labels.

The only request is that you please apply in good faith. Please be honest and do not apply if you do not identify as queer/lgbtqia+ or you are not a person of color.
