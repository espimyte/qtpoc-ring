Rules & Guidelines

  1. You should only apply if you are a queer/lgbtqia+ person of color.
  2. Your website should have some content on it. Even a small amount of content, like an about me, is acceptable.
  3. Your page should not promote hateful content or bigotry.
  4. That's it!

Please note that you may be denied/removed for reasons not explicitly stated here.

Submission Form

If you wish to join, fill out the following form. If you've provided an email, you will be notified on whether or not you've been accepted.
